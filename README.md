# 📚StudentAI - A smart AI powered portal for students
The student portal in which students can plan their exams, upcoming tasks, suggested by ai, and also an personalised ai powered chatbot for mental health.

## 🧠Tech Stack
- Python, Flask Restful
- Postgresql
- HTML
- CSS (Tailwind css)
- Js
## 🔥Features
- Smart Exam planner: Give your exam schedule, let AI plan for you.
- To do list: Your tasks, at one place, you can add or remove or complete a task
- AI chatbot: For students in stress or any mental issues, get rid of them with help of the chatbot
